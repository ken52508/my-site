About

I'm an EE master's student working on speckle image classification generated by a voltage-tunable PDLC (polymer-dispersed liquid crystal) optical scattering system. My thesis focuses on how classification reliability holds up across different data-acquisition sessions and model architectures — not just whether a model can hit a high accuracy number, but whether that number can be trusted.

I'm currently exploring roles in customer solutions engineering (CSE), field application engineering (FAE), application engineering, and technical program/product management — anywhere that sits close to both the hardware and the customer, and where careful, evidence-based troubleshooting matters as much as technical depth.

關於我

我是電機碩士生，研究主題是用電壓可調的 PDLC（聚合物分散液晶）光學散射系統產生的散斑影像分類。我的碩論關注的不只是模型能不能跑出高準確率，而是這個準確率在不同資料取得階段、不同模型架構下，還撐不撐得住——也就是分類結果的可信度邊界在哪裡。

目前在探索的方向包括客戶解決方案工程（CSE）、現場應用工程（FAE）、應用工程，以及技術專案/產品管理——只要是同時貼近硬體與客戶、需要嚴謹且以證據為本的排查能力的角色，都是我感興趣的方向。
